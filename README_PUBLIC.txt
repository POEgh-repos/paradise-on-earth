WHAT IS PARADISE ON EARTH?
African creative NFT marketplace and social platform.
Buy, collect and flex digital artworks from African
musicians, fashion designers, upcyclers and visual
artists. Pay with MTN MoMo — no crypto knowledge needed.

GETTING STARTED
1. Visit paradise-on-earth.vercel.app on any device
2. Tap SIGN IN → Continue with Google
3. Your profile is created automatically
4. A Polygon wallet address is assigned to you
5. Start collecting and posting

FEATURES
◈ FEED       — See what the community is collecting
◉ EXPLORE    — Browse all NFT collections and pieces
◆ CREATORS   — Follow your favourite African artists
◇ WALLET     — Your owned NFT collection + wallet
△ PROFILE    — Set your name, zodiac, bio and photo
🔔 NOTIFS    — Real-time activity notifications

BUYING AN NFT
1. Tap any NFT piece
2. Tap "Buy with MoMo"
3. Enter your MTN MoMo number
4. Approve the payment prompt on your phone
5. The NFT lands in your wallet automatically

POSTING
1. Sign in → go to Feed
2. Tap the composer box
3. Write your post — use @username to mention people
   and #hashtag to tag topics
4. Add photos or videos (tap ◎)
5. Tap POST

YOUR ZODIAC AVATAR
Set your zodiac sign in Profile.
Your avatar alternates between your photo and your
zodiac symbol every 3 seconds everywhere on the platform.

POLYGON WALLET
A Polygon wallet address is auto-assigned when you
sign in. View it in the Wallet page — tap to copy.
No seed phrases. No crypto setup. Just collect.

NOTIFICATIONS
Real-time bell icon in the top nav. Notifies you when:
- Someone likes or comments on your post
- You receive a new NFT
- Admin sends you a notice

MODERATION
Content on PARADISE is moderated by GOD🤎.
Verified creators get 👑 next to their name.
